How do I get the video link from Twitter / X?

On the Twitter (X) app or website, locate the post containing the video. Click or tap the "Share" button beneath the post, then choose "Copy link". Paste this link into our search box above.

Can I download GIFs from Twitter (X)?

Yes! Twitter encodes GIFs as looping MP4 videos. When you download a Twitter GIF with XSave, you get an ultra-high quality MP4 that plays seamlessly on all devices.

Can I download videos from private Twitter accounts?

No. In order to protect user privacy and respect platform terms, XSave only downloads publicly accessible tweets and posts.
