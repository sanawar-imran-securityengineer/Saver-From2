Safety & Privacy

Built with security-first principles for public content only.

Public URLs Only

Only publicly accessible YouTube videos. No private or restricted content.

No Login Required

Zero passwords, cookies, session tokens, or API keys — ever.

Auto-Delete in 30 min

All files are automatically purged within 30 minutes to protect your privacy.

UUID Filenames

Rate Limited

Terms & Usage

Acceptable Use

Use SwiftFetch to download publicly accessible YouTube videos you own or have explicit permission to download. Do not use it to download private, restricted, paid, or age-restricted content.

Your Responsibility

You are solely responsible for ensuring your use complies with all applicable copyright laws, YouTube's Terms of Service, and third-party rights. SwiftFetch does not verify ownership.

No Warranties

This service is provided "as is". Downloads may fail if YouTube changes its systems or a video is unavailable in the requested format. A lower quality may be used as fallback.

Privacy & Security

No Personal Data Stored

SwiftFetch requires no account, login, or personal information. No passwords, cookies, session IDs, or access tokens are ever requested or stored.

Temporary Storage Only

Downloaded files are stored temporarily and automatically deleted after 30 minutes. No files are kept permanently.

No Analytics

SwiftFetch does not use external analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party tracking. Your IP is used only for rate limiting, not stored long-term.
