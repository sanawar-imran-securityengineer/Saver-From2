Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Notice & Policy

Compliance Protocol 17 U.S.C. § 512 • Updated September 2026 • Respect for Creator Rights

Statement of Principles

OmniDownloader strictly respects the intellectual property rights of content creators, film producers, independent artists, and recording musicians. We expect all users of our utility to exercise similar respect for creative rights.

It is our policy to respond expeditiously to clear, valid notices of alleged copyright infringement complying with the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA") and international intellectual property treaties.

ℹ️
Important Architectural Clarification

OmniDownloader does not store, host, mirror, or publish any video or audio files on our servers. All videos remain on the servers of their original hosts (e.g., YouTube, TikTok, Meta). When a user inputs a URL, our gateway functions as a real-time data pipe delivering the public stream directly to the requester's device.

Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner, or authorized to act on behalf of one, and you believe that media accessible through OmniDownloader infringes your protected work, you may submit a formal written notice containing the following mandatory elements:

  1. Physical or Electronic Signature: The signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the Copyrighted Work: A clear description of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, including registration numbers if applicable.
  3. Identification of Infringing URLs: The exact URLs on the host platform (e.g., specific YouTube or TikTok links) that you request our service block from extraction.
  4. Contact Information: Your legal name, organization, physical mailing address, telephone number, and valid email address.
  5. Good Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Statement of Accuracy Under Penalty of Perjury: A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf.

Designated DMCA Agent Contact

Formal notices must be directed to our designated agent via email:

DMCA Agent: OmniDownloader IP Compliance Dept.
Email: legal@saverfrom.local
Subject Line: [DMCA Notice] Formal Infringement Notification

Please note that under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing may be subject to civil liability and statutory damages.

Domain & URL Blacklisting Enforcement

Upon receipt of a valid and verified infringement notice, OmniDownloader takes immediate technical measures to permanently blacklist the specified URLs or domains from our extraction gateway, preventing future requests from resolving that media stream.
