3. Technical Differences: Spotlight Clips vs Ephemeral Public Stories

Snapchat categorizes public media into two primary architectures:

  • Spotlight Media: Spotlight clips are persistent, public audiovisual creations hosted on Snap’s global media servers. They do not expire after 24 hours and can be fetched reliably at any time.
  • Public Stories: Stories posted by public figures, verified creators, and Snap Map contributions have a strict 24-hour expiration lifecycle. Once the 24-hour window closes, Snap’s origin servers purge the manifest, after which the video cannot be downloaded.
