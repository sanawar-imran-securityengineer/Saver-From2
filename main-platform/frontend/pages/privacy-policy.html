Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026 • Strict Zero-Logs Guarantee
🛡️
The Short & Honest Summary

We do not track you. We do not store your IP address. We do not log which videos or media you download. We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to brokers. OmniDownloader is a purely transient utility.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike conventional media converters that build profiles on user browsing habits, OmniDownloader is designed under privacy-by-design principles:

  • No Personal Identification: We never ask for your name, phone number, physical address, or payment details.
  • No Account Requirements: You never need to sign up, log in, or link third-party social media profiles.
  • No Activity Logging: We do not keep records linking your IP address to URLs submitted to our download engine. When a request completes, the URL reference is immediately discarded from working RAM.
  • No Media Retention: We do not store, mirror, or archive downloaded videos or audio files on our servers. Media is streamed directly through volatile proxy buffers.

2. Technical Information Processed During Download

To deliver our service securely and prevent automated denial-of-service (DoS) attacks, our edge servers process transient network metadata:

  • HTTP Headers & User Agent: Transmitted by your browser to ensure media formats (MP4, MP3, WebM) are compatible with your operating system and device.
  • Real-time Rate Limiting: High-frequency automated scrapers are rate-limited using in-memory counters that expire continuously. No permanent IP database is compiled.
  • Error Diagnostic Telemetry: In the event of an extractor failure (e.g., a changed YouTube API endpoint), anonymous error codes and platform identifiers are collected to alert engineers. No user identity is attached.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

OmniDownloader does not use tracking cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-site analytics beacons.

We may use minimal client-side browser LocalStorage exclusively for user-interface preferences (such as saving your preferred audio vs. video toggle, or dark/light theme state). This data stays entirely on your local computer and is never communicated to our servers.

4. End-to-End Transport Security (HTTPS)

All interactions between your browser and OmniDownloader are secured using modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic encryption. This prevents Internet Service Providers (ISPs), public Wi-Fi operators, or network snoops from intercepting URLs or file payloads you request through our portal.

5. Third-Party Platform Links

OmniDownloader extracts media published on third-party networks (including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Meta, X/Twitter, Reddit, Pinterest, and Twitch). Once you leave our domain or interact with external services, their respective privacy policies and terms govern your data.

6. Policy Updates & Inquiries

We reserve the right to revise this policy to reflect infrastructural changes or legal standards. Revisions will be posted here with an updated effective date. For questions regarding our privacy architecture, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@saverfrom.local.
